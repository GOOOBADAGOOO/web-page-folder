Triple Nacho Amir Delivery Service

To keep our customers protected and fed as fast as possible, The Triple Nacho runs a highly exclusive, in-house delivery service. We do not use third-party apps. Instead, we rely on a single, legendary delivery hero.

The Fleet: 1 Delivery Vehicle

The Driver: Amir

Amir is the undisputed, perfect delivery driver. He has a flawless driving record, never spills a drop of The Bombing Cheese, and handles every order with maximum care. He knows every shortcut, alleyway, and side street in the city by heart.

While Amir is always fast, he has a legendary superpower known to all the local regulars. If a customer leaves a tip of $1 or higher on their order, Amir unlocks his ultimate form and delivers the food as fast as light. The second the order drops into his delivery bag, he transforms into a streak of lightning, arriving at your doorstep before the cheese sauce can even think about cooling down.

With Amir on the road, your nachos are guaranteed to arrive perfectly crunchy, steaming hot, and protected from the elements every single time.
