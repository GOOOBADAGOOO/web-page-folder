The Story Of Nachos

Nachos are a popular snack that started in Northern Mexico, specifically in a town called Piedras Negras. They were created by Ignacio Anaya in 1943 at a place called the Old Victory Club. One day, when Anaya saw some hungry military wives, he decided to fry up some tortilla chips and top them with cheese and jalapeños. He named his creation “Nachos Especials.” The military wives loved them so much that they ended up in a church cookbook in Eagle Pass. What began as a local favorite quickly spread beyond borders, and now nachos are enjoyed all over the world, with different toppings to please everyone’s taste.

Nachos have come a long way since their simple beginnings, and they remain a favorite at restaurants and home gatherings.
