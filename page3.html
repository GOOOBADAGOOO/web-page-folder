Types Of The Triple Nachos

1. The Alexander Memorial Nachos

The Recipe: A bed of house-fried blue corn tortilla chips topped with 14-hour slow-braised beef birria. The chips are smothered in a warm, melted smoked-gouda queso and finished with pickled red onions and fresh cilantro. It comes with a side of rich beef consommé broth for dipping.

The Review: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "Dipping a cheesy, beef-loaded blue corn chip into that warm birria broth is a total game-changer." — Marcus T.

2. Aiden’s Hot Honey & Carnitas Nachos

The Recipe: Sweet potato tortilla chips piled high with crispy, citrus-marinated pulled pork (carnitas). The nachos are drizzled with a house-made habanero hot honey and topped with grilled pineapple pico de gallo, crumbled cotija cheese, and a swirl of cool avocado crema.

The Review: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "The sweet pork mixed with the spicy habanero honey and grilled pineapple is the perfect balance of sweet and heat!" — Sarah L.

3. Christopher’s Street Corn Nachos

The Recipe: Crisp white corn chips layered with charred, smoky sweet corn cut straight off the cob. It is coated in a spicy lime-garlic aioli, dusted heavily with tajin spice, and loaded with melted jack cheese and fresh jalapeño slices.

The Review: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "The smoky corn and garlic aioli make these nachos taste exactly like authentic Mexican street corn." — Elena R.

4. The Unbreakable Trio Volcano Nachos

The Recipe: A massive, shareable dish using a blend of all three tortilla chips (blue corn, white corn, and sweet potato) stacked high like a volcano. It features layers of smoky three-cheese queso, shredded chipotle chicken, black beans, and roasted jalapeños. It is served with three dipping bowls in the center: fresh guacamole, salsa verde, and ghost pepper salsa.

The Review: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "Massive and perfect for a group. The three separate dips mean everyone gets what they want." — David K.
